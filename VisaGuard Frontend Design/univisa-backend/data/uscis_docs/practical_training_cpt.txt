Curricular Practical Training (CPT)

F students are eligible for curricular practical training (CPT) at either the undergraduate or graduate level during the program of study.

CPT employment must:

• Be an integral part of an established curriculum.
• Directly relate to the student's major area of study.

A student who has been authorized for 12 months or more of full-time CPT at the same program level is not eligible for OPT.

Authorization for CPT is made by the DSO in SEVIS.
