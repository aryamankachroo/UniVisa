Employment Authorization and EAD Framework

Application for Employment Authorization

Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, is used to request employment authorization.

i-765

Employment Authorization Document (EAD)

The EAD (Form I-766) is issued as evidence that the holder is authorized to work in the United States.

i-765instr

Initial EAD – Issued for the first time under a specific eligibility category.
Renewal EAD – Issued after expiration of a previous EAD under the same category.
Replacement EAD – Issued when a previously issued EAD was lost, stolen, damaged, or contains errors.

i-765instr

Form I-765WS

Form I-765WS (Worksheet) is required for applicants under certain deferred action categories to demonstrate economic necessity.
