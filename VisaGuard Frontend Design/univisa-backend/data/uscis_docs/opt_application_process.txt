OPT Application Process and Filing Requirements

OPT Recommendation and Filing Timeline

Students must not file Form I-765 until the DSO has recommended OPT in SEVIS and issued an updated Form I-20 with the OPT recommendation.

The student must file Form I-765 within 30 days of the DSO's OPT recommendation.

If the Form I-765 is filed before the DSO recommendation or after the 30-day period:

• USCIS will deny the application.
• The student will lose the filing fee.
• The student must reapply and pay the fee again.

OPT Request Statuses in SEVIS

Requested – DSO entered OPT recommendation; USCIS has not yet received Form I-765.
Pending – USCIS has received Form I-765.
Approved – USCIS approved Form I-765.
Denied – USCIS denied Form I-765.
Canceled – OPT request canceled in SEVIS.
Withdrawn – USCIS approved withdrawal of Form I-765.

Form I-765

Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, is used to request employment authorization and an Employment Authorization Document (EAD).

i-765

Purpose of Form I-765

Certain foreign nationals in the United States may file Form I-765 to request employment authorization and an EAD.

Employment Authorization Document (EAD)

The EAD is Form I-766 issued as evidence that the holder is authorized to work in the United States.

Students must enter the appropriate eligibility category in Part 2, Item 27 of Form I-765.
