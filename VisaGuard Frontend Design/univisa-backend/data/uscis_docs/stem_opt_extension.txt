STEM OPT Extension – 24 Month Extension

24-Month STEM OPT Extension

For students who majored in designated Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) degree programs approved by DHS, a 24-month extension of OPT is available.

Regulatory authority includes 8 CFR 214.2(f)(10) through (13).

The STEM OPT extension is an extension of post-completion OPT and requires:

• DSO recommendation in SEVIS
• Filing of Form I-765 with USCIS
• USCIS approval and issuance of EAD

Students must not begin employment under the STEM extension until the appropriate authorization period begins as reflected on the EAD.
